# Moving to the US

## Day 1 - Moving and Meeting

I packed up all of my stuff into 2 checked bags, a carry-on and a personal item. Initially I was going to fly from Victoria to Vancouver to Chicago to Burlington and arrive at 9pm, but my Vancouver to Chicago flight got delayed and I had to rebook from Vancouver to Washington to Burlington and would arrive at 10am the next day instead. So after getting $50 in meal vouchers from United I ate a chicken and kimchi bowl alongside 2 smoothies, and slept for 6 hours at the airport. My next 2 flights weren't that eventful but my flight to Burlington was kinda nice, and was a smaller little plane with really friendly people on board. So I flew into Vermont where Olive picked me up, and then we drove over to Julia's place where I'm staying at for the next 2 weeks :yay-cat: we just chilled and had Jonathan over because he was leaving the next day, and then Mihi and Ren pulled up so I got to meet a couple gappies too! My luggage got absolutely violated by TSA so I had to cleanup all my hardware and lots of printed parts broke in my foldup printer, so it was a good test for vibrations lol. Now I'm wrapping up my day with a bit of drawing and also setting up my YSWS budget and some other stuff.

## Day 2 - Planning and Arangements

Woke up kind of late at 10pm but it was chill because it was a weekend and then me and Julia made our FIRE apple crisp which will sustain us for YEARSSSS now. Then we did some work and then headed to a board game cafe right by the river where we played some fun games and overspent on food :D after that we kind of chilled because I had a lot of work to do in finding my lease so I'm not homeless in September, setting up a US bank account and figuring out how to get my SSN. For supper, big chef Julia made some teriyaki which was low-key so good and then we all locked in. Did some more art which I’ll maybe post a bit of when it's not embarrassing but SOON!!

## Day 3 - First day of work!

Day 3 of living in Vermont as a gappie :D I went to sleep wayyyyyy too late at like 2 - 3am, so I only got like 6 hours of sleep for my first day of work, but it was my fault because I just stared at the ceiling for a couple hours too long, but it was very therapeutic. So I woke up at 8:20 and quickly got ready, and finished off the apple crisp so tomorrow's breakfast won't slap as hard :(( I got to work perfectly on time, and got to meet a couple of the gappies and then we were ushered into one of the meeting rooms to get onboarded. Onboarding was really nice, Hack Club has this internal bridge tool, which we just plug our [hackclub.com](http://hackclub.com) email into, and then it adds us to all the channels, sets up our loops, fillout, airtable, EVERYTHING, so it was pretty easy to get setup. Olive and Augie were guiding us through the process so it was really chill, but GitHub lowk had an outage during our onboarding so it took a bit longer than we wanted. Gap years are really autonomous, so we were just told to read the docs and then get right into our program. It was a bit more complicated because we still needed to talk to quite a few people to get some more niche permissions setup, but it wasn't too much of a hassle. Time flew by but I managed to get a little form connected to my airtable that's connected to the unified DB, so I'm kind of ready to run my program now which is going to be SO INSANELY HYPE. We also had this little meeting, we have a couple scheduled per week where we do like goals and stuff, and updates about our programs, so I was just getting used to the day-by-days. I left a little bit longer to talk with some of the other gappies, and then I went over to Liam Newbills's place to see what it was like, and then just headed back, where supper was already being prepared by froppii and estella :D And then we kind of just chilled, a bit of karaoke, and finishing up with some work! So hyped for my program release tomorrow, it's going to be awesome
